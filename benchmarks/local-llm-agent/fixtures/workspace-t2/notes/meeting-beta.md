# Tessier supplier quality review

## Summary
Ravi and Petra reviewed the Tessier encoder batch data with Mara. The failing encoders
all come from the March lot stamped TQ-117. Tessier offered a replacement lot at no cost
but asked for a relaxed delivery window. The team accepted the replacement lot and set an
incoming-inspection sampling plan, while keeping the v2.4 software compensation as the
field fix.

## Decisions
- Accept Tessier's no-cost replacement for lot TQ-117 with a delivery window extended to eight weeks.
- Introduce incoming inspection with a 5% sampling plan for all future encoder lots.
- Keep the v2.4 software compensation enabled in the field even after the replacement lot ships.
