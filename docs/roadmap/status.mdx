<Note>This page is the honest, current state — updated as we go, never aspirational.
Last verified against the tree at **v0.12.0** (2026-07-05).</Note>

**Shipped and released (0.12.0):** the full three-plane platform — meetings
(bots on [gmeet/teams/zoom-web](/api/meetings), transcription → `transcript.v1`,
[durable transcript persistence](/architecture/transcript-persistence), recordings),
the agent plane ([`unit.v1` dispatch](/concepts) in runtime-spawned isolated workers over
bind-mounted [workspaces](/concepts#workspace), per-dispatch signed
[identity](/architecture/identity-and-trust) tokens, durable chat memory), the
[terminal workbench](/clients/terminal), three proven deploy targets
([compose](/deployment), [lite](/deployment/lite), [helm](/deployment/helm)) shipping from
published images, the [config.v1 deployment contract](/configuration) with `/health`
capability rows, [webhooks](/how-to/webhooks), [token scoping](/authentication), MCP.
See the [changelog](/changelog) for the release inventory and the
[operating principles](/principles) for how the stack surfaces and prevents faults.

**Designed & frozen (this site):** the [seven primitives](/concepts), the
[identity layer](/architecture/identity-and-trust) (kagenti-aligned), the
[governance model](/architecture/governance), the
[multi-workspace collaboration direction](/roadmap/multi-workspace).

**Next:** the [0.12.1 fast-follow and the 0.12.x arc](/roadmap/next) — speaker attribution
on Teams/Zoom, the transcript share surface, docs verification sealing, and the
multi-workspace arc.
